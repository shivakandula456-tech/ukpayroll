Questions

Questions, answered.

Practical answers about this service. If your question is not covered, talk to our team.

Ask our team
What is the difference between white label and direct payroll?

With white label payroll, your practice presents the service and we process it behind your brand. With direct delivery, ActualPay provides the payroll service to your client. Both models can coexist, and the right choice depends on how you want to manage the client relationship.

How is client payroll data handled?

We agree a secure route for payroll inputs, approvals and reports before the first run. Access is limited to the people who need it, and confidentiality requirements are included in the service arrangement.

Can you support our existing payroll software?

During scoping we review your current systems, the outputs your clients need and how data is shared. Where a fully managed service is used, we will explain which systems sit behind the process and what your team needs to provide each period.
