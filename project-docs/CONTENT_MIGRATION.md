# Content migration

**Migration date:** 21 October 2026  
**Source:** the verified ActualPay UK content inventory and source-site review recorded during the rebuild.

## Principles

- Every known public route is retained at its original path.
- The eight service pages, six sector descriptions, 15 FAQs, nine articles, contact details, opening hours, About Us content and supplied testimonials are represented.
- Body content was preserved in substance. Headlines, ordering and presentation were edited for clarity, UK spelling and a consistent page hierarchy.
- No pricing, service-level guarantee, customer name, testimonial, credential or performance statistic was invented.
- Legal text was carried forward; it still requires ActualPay's legal review before launch.
- No source page was redirected. The 404 page is excluded from the sitemap.

## Documented changes

- The approved hero redesign replaced the original static hero with the supplied video plus a responsive poster and conditional loading. The original body content below the hero remains.
- SEO titles were shortened for the accounting category, Black Friday article and bookkeeping article; the duplicate payroll category title was corrected. Meta descriptions were adjusted for length and clarity. See `SEO_REPORT.md`.
- Three category archives were added to connect articles that already existed in the source inventory.
- The contact and quote forms now validate in the browser and prepare an email in the visitor's mail app. This is a temporary fallback; no production form provider is configured or approved. No submission is stored or falsely reported as sent.
- Image files were converted/re-encoded as WebP where used on-page, and the article cover on the payroll guide now uses responsive WebP sources. Open Graph images remain 1200×630 JPEG.
- The hero video was re-encoded from 1920×1080 / 3.9 MB to 1600×900 / 1.56 MB, and mobile/reduced-motion visitors do not download it.
- No known URL changed. No server-side redirect is implemented or claimed.
