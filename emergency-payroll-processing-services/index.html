When to call

Payroll problems rarely arrive at a convenient time

An emergency payroll is usually needed because something outside the normal process has broken. The sooner we understand the situation, the sooner we can assess what is possible before the pay date.

Emergency payroll is not a guaranteed same-day service. What can be achieved depends on payroll data quality, access to records and the time before payday. We will tell you honestly what is possible.

  • A payroll employee has left and cover cannot be arranged in time
  • Payroll software has failed or access has been lost
  • A deadline has been missed or data arrived late
  • A payroll provider is unresponsive and payday is approaching

Questions

Questions, answered.

Practical answers about this service. If your question is not covered, talk to our team.

Ask our team
Can you run payroll today?

That depends on when you contact us, the information available and the size and complexity of the payroll. We do not promise same-day delivery. Call us as early as possible and we will give you a direct assessment of what can be achieved.

What if we do not have all the payroll records?

Tell us what you do have: previous payslips, bank payment records, accounting entries, HMRC correspondence or backups. We will assess whether a reliable payroll can be rebuilt. Missing data may limit what can be completed and must be handled carefully.

Can you take over ongoing payroll afterwards?

Yes. Many emergency engagements become an ongoing payroll outsourcing or bureau arrangement once the immediate pressure is resolved. You can decide after the urgent work whether you want us to continue.
