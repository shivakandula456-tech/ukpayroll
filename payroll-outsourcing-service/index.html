What it is

A payroll function without the in-house overhead

Payroll outsourcing means asking a specialist provider to manage some or all of your payroll process. Instead of relying on one person’s software knowledge or squeezing payroll between other tasks, you gain an established team, a repeatable process and a clear point of contact.

ActualPay handles the calculations, payslips, HMRC submissions and associated reporting. You approve the inputs and stay close to the output; we keep the pay run moving and the compliance tasks on schedule.

  • A complete service from starters and leavers to year-end
  • Support for weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or monthly payroll
  • One team for payroll, pensions and HMRC reporting
  • Scope and fees agreed before your first pay run

What’s included

A comprehensive outsourced payroll service

The final scope is tailored to your business. Typical payroll outsourcing work includes:

Payroll processing

  • New starter and leaver administration
  • Timesheet, overtime and variable pay input
  • Gross-to-net calculations and deductions
  • Statutory sick, maternity, paternity, shared parental and adoption pay
  • Holiday accrual and leave calculations
  • Payslip production and distribution
  • P45s, P60s and payroll record keeping

Compliance and reporting

  • PAYE scheme registration and Government Gateway agency support
  • Real Time Information submissions to HMRC
  • PAYE and National Insurance calculations
  • Pension scheme integration, including NEST, and contribution schedules
  • P11D and PSA reporting, plus CIS subcontractor submissions
  • Payroll journals and management reports
  • Year-end processing and P60 production
  • Support with payroll queries and HMRC correspondence

Getting started

A measured move to outsourced payroll

The exact onboarding plan depends on your current setup, but the usual path looks like this.

  1. 01

  2. 02

    Agree the scope

Questions

Questions, answered.

Practical answers about this service. If your question is not covered, talk to our team.

Ask our team
Can you run weekly and monthly payroll?

We agree the payroll frequency and pay dates during onboarding. Payroll can be set up around weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or monthly cycles, including more than one cycle if your workforce needs it.

Do we need to buy payroll software?

For a fully managed service, you do not need to buy payroll software for the processing itself. We discuss your existing systems, reporting requirements and how data should be shared during the quotation process.

How is payroll data shared securely?

We agree a suitable method for sending payroll inputs and receiving reports before the first pay run. Employee data is confidential, so access is limited to the people who need it. Specific security arrangements are confirmed in your service agreement.
