The payroll cycle

What comprehensive payroll support covers

The exact scope is always agreed before work starts, but a full payroll service can include the tasks that keep your pay runs moving and your obligations met.

That can mean routine processing, HMRC filings, pension assessments, year-end reporting and the payroll records that sit behind them.

  • Onboarding and payroll set-up
  • Starter and leaver processing
  • Timesheet and variable pay input
  • Tax, National Insurance and deductions
  • Payslip production and distribution
  • RTI submissions to HMRC
  • Statutory payments and leave
  • P45s, P60s and year-end support
  • Pension assessments and contributions
  • Payroll reports for finance teams
  • CIS and P11D/PSA support
  • Payroll queries and guidance

Questions

Questions, answered.

Practical answers about choosing and combining payroll services.

Ask our team
Can the services be combined?

Yes. A business may use payroll outsourcing alongside pension and P11D support, or an accountancy practice may choose white label processing for some clients and direct support for others. We agree the scope so responsibilities stay clear.

Can you work with our current payroll software?

During scoping we review your current systems, data-sharing requirements and reporting needs. Where a fully managed service is used, we explain which systems sit behind the process and what your team needs to provide each period.

How is pricing agreed?

Pricing is based on the work involved, including headcount, pay frequency, the services selected and any specialist reporting. We confirm the scope and fee basis in writing before the first pay run.
